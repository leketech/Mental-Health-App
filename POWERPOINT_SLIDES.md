# 📋 PowerPoint Presentation Guide

## Slide Design Tips:
1. **Use consistent branding** - Green/teal color scheme throughout
2. **Include app screenshots** - Show actual product interfaces
3. **Add animations** - Subtle transitions for key points
4. **Use high-quality images** - Professional photos and graphics
5. **Keep text readable** - Large fonts, high contrast

## Presentation Flow:
- **Slides 1-3**: Hook the audience with the problem and solution
- **Slides 4-10**: Demonstrate the actual product with screenshots
- **Slides 11-13**: Present market opportunity and competitive advantage  
- **Slides 14-16**: Build credibility with success stories and team
- **Slides 17-19**: Show growth strategy and investment opportunity
- **Slides 20-22**: Strong call-to-action and contact information

## Interactive Elements:
- **Live Demo** during slides 4-10 (if internet available)
- **QR Codes** for audience to try the app
- **Q&A Breaks** after market opportunity and product demo sections

This presentation structure allows you to showcase your app effectively while building a compelling investment case! 🚀
